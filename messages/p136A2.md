# p136A2

Childs page 133, 5 November 1918. Key nov04. Status Solved, 1 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 1 open position. The ciphertext shown is the page reading, 452 symbols where the log transcription shows 447.
