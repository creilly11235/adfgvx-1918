# p212B

Childs page 209, 19 November 1918. Key nov19. Status Solved. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017.

Detail. Period key, as transcribed, confidence high.

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 209, which is page 212 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 212. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `B) IWE v LB (07.31 p.m., November 19th)`. The heading counts 310 symbols and the log shows 310.
