# p221B3

Childs page 218, 25 November 1918. Key nov25. Status Solved, 4 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 4 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the log transcription with the registered edits applied. The log transcription holds 205 cells with no printed count, so a block of five cells was left out, at a place chosen by score where a nearby place changes three letters. No second reading of the page was made for this record. No page reading has placed any of these edits. Letters where the other close repairs decode differently are counted as open. The digits are read as numerals and not counted. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 4 open letters at most, letters 62, 77, 88 and 100, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
