# p190D1

Childs page 187, 13 November 1918. Key nov13_20. Status Partially solved. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence first 50 positions that do not read, then a reading of 76 positions with 4 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the log transcription with the registered edits applied. The log transcription holds 248 cells against the count of 252 noted in the log, from one reading, and one group has six symbols, its first symbol unread. That unread symbol was dropped and five unknown cells were added near the end, a choice made by score where no page reading places either edit. Without these edits the page cells do not read. The first 50 positions do not read and are shown as undetermined, with a few short German words between runs of unknown letters. The 76 positions after them read as German with 4 open letters, letters 95, 105, 117 and 124, and the round trip over every symbol of the text shown passes under the published key. Letters where the other close repairs decode differently are counted as open. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
