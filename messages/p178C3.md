# p178C3

Childs page 175, 10 November 1918. Key nov10. Status Solved, 9 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 9 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the page reading with the added cells marked as unknown. The page holds 217 cells against the printed count of 222, so five unknown cells were inserted before cell 99, a place chosen by score and not settled by the page, and the letters after it depend on that choice. The digit is read as a numeral and not counted. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 9 open letters at most, letters 6, 27, 31, 35, 39, 43, 47, 51 and 59, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
