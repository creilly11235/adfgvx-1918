# p207B

Childs page 204, 17 November 1918. Key nov16. Status Solved. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017.

Detail. Period key, as transcribed, confidence high.

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 204, which is page 207 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 207. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `B) WAU, IWE v,LP (6 p.m., November 17th)`. The heading counts 92 symbols and the log shows 92.

## 5 Decrypted after repair

Uppercase letters rest on symbols that are in the print. Lowercase letters rest on the repair. `_` is a letter not determined and `#` a digit not determined. X stands for a space or a stop.
