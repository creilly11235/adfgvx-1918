# p091B

Childs page 88, 1 November 1918. Key nov01. Status Solved, 4 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading from two blind readings of the page, confidence reading with 4 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the collation of the two page witnesses, 204 symbols where the log transcription shows 200, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 4 open letters at most. None of them is located, since all 4 were counted by hand without a position, so the count is an upper bound and can fall. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
