# p188_

Childs page 185, 13 November 1918. Key nov13_20. Status Solved, 32 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 32 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the log transcription with the registered edits applied. The log transcription holds 279 cells against the printed count of 268, so two blocks of five cells and one more cell were left out, at places chosen by score. Another close repair gives the same message without its first ten letters and with ten new letters at the end that do not resolve into words, so the first ten letters are counted as open together with the letters where the close repairs differ. Most letters of the text depend on those choices. No page reading has placed any of these edits. Letters where the other close repairs decode differently are counted as open. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 32 open letters at most, letters 1 to 10, 15, 19, 25, 29, 35, 45, 48, 55, 58, 65, 75, 93, 95, 105, 113 to 117, 123, 125 and 133, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
