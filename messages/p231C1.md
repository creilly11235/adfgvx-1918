# p231C1

Childs page 228, 30 November 1918. Key nov28. Status Read. Read with the published keys.

Detail. Period key, as transcribed, confidence high. Read with Lasry's published keys (Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker 2017).

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 228, which is page 231 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 231. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `C) BSL v 20N (04.47 asm., Deceuber lst)`, part 1. The heading counts 176 symbols and the log shows 176.
