# p182D3

Childs page 179, 11 November 1918. Key nov13_16. Status Partially solved. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading from two blind readings of the page, confidence reading of the first 87 positions with no open position, then 19 positions that do not read. The first 87 positions read as German up to a sign off. The 19 positions after it do not read as German and are shown as undetermined. Six of them sit on cells of the key square that are not known, so they may be letters or digits. The log text holds no symbols for this entry, so the whole transcription comes from the page. Whether they are a reference number or code group is a guess. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
