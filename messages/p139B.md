# p139B

Childs page 136, 6 November 1918. Key nov04. Status Read. Read with Lasry's published keys.

Detail. Period key, as transcribed, confidence high. Read with Lasry's published keys (Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker 2017).

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 136, which is page 139 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 139. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `B) NKJ v LP (6.16 p.m., November 6th)`. The heading counts 304 symbols and the log shows 314.
