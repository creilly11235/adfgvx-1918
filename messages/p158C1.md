# p158C1

Childs page 155, 7 November 1918. Key nov07. Status Solved, 9 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 9 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the log transcription with the registered edits applied. The log transcription holds 186 cells with no printed count, and one group has six symbols. One symbol of that group was dropped and one unknown cell added at the end, both chosen by score, and the letters after them depend on that choice. No second reading of the page was made for this record. No page reading has placed any of these edits. Letters where the other close repairs decode differently are counted as open. The digits are read as numerals and not counted. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 9 open letters at most, letters 1, 25, 54 and 88 to 93, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
