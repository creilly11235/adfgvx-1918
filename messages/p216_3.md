# p216_3

Childs page 213, 23 November 1918. Key nov22. Status Partially solved. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading of the first 40 positions with no open position, then 41 positions that do not read. Two readings of the page agree on all 162 cells, three of them unread on both, and the count equals the sheet label, which was reread as 162 where the log label gives 168, so no edit was made. The first 40 positions read as German with no open letter, and the round trip over every symbol of the text shown passes under the published key. The 41 positions after them do not read and look like padding or a signature and are shown as undetermined. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 213, which is page 216 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 216. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `A)KOF v LP (06.20 p.m., Noveinoer 23rd)`, part 3. The heading counts 168 symbols and the log shows 163.

## 6 English translation

German in phrases.

```
FUER EUCH WIRD MIT ALLEN KRAEFTEN GESORGT X ROHA
____________________________ # ____________
```

for you is with all strength provided for unclear [undetermined?] [number?] [undetermined?]

A short message saying that provision is being made for the recipients with all strength. The first 40 positions read and the 41 after them do not read and look like padding or a signature, so they are shown as undetermined. The text is a best guess.
