# p220C.2

Childs page 217, 24 November 1918. Key dec09_history. Status Read. Credited to Prinz, 2026.

Detail. Period key, as transcribed, confidence high. Prinz, September 2026, with a key from Childs 1919 typescript. Our decode reads under the key too.

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 217, which is page 220 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 220. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `C) LP v NKJ (01.27 a.u., Noveuber 27th)`. The heading counts 170 symbols and the log shows 170.
