# p165B1

Childs page 162, 8 November 1918. Key nov07. Status Solved, 7 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 7 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the page reading with the added cells marked as unknown. The page shows one cell fewer than the printed count of 182, so one unknown cell was added at the head, a placement the page does not settle. From the middle on the text is letter and digit code groups, and the digits are read as numerals and not counted. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 7 open letters at most, letters 3, 13, 39, 44, 48 to 49 and 64, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
