# p115A2

Childs page 112, 3 November 1918. Key nov01. Status Solved, 20 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading from two blind readings of the page, confidence reading with 20 open positions. The page symbols were read twice blind and agree on every letter, so the ciphertext shown is the agreed page reading. Open positions are marked in the text. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 112, which is page 115 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 115. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `A) --- v 20N (2.33 a.m., November 4th)`, part 2. The heading counts 218 symbols and the log shows 203.
