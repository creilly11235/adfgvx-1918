# p220A.3

Childs page 217, 24 November 1918. Key nov22. Status Solved, 14 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 14 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the page reading, 122 symbols where the log transcription shows 115. Two readings of the page agree on all 122 cells, which equals the printed count, and the log transcription lacks seven of them, so no edit was made. The last twelve letters, one of them a digit, read as padding or a signature and are counted as open, together with two earlier places where the word the text needs is not the letter the key gives. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 14 open letters at most, letters 24, 46 and 50 to 61, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
