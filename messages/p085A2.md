# p085A2

Childs page 82, 30 October 1918. Key oct28. Status Solved. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017.

Detail. Period key, length repaired, confidence high after repair.

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 82, which is page 85 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 85. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `A) LP v NKJ (6.10 p.m., October 30th)`, part 2. The heading counts 248 symbols and the log shows 249.
