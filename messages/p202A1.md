# p202A1

Childs page 199, 15 November 1918. Key nov13_16. Status Solved, 32 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 32 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the log transcription with one cell near the end taken from the two page readings, which agree against the log there, and with one cell dropped, 292 symbols, which equals the printed count. The log transcription and both page readings hold 293 cells against the printed count of 292, so one cell was dropped, at a place chosen by score among the 51 places that pass the gate and not by the page, and 70 of the 146 letters depend on that choice. The ten drops nearest it decode the same message and differ at ten letters, which are counted as open. One letter of the date and a stretch of 25 letters that reads as a list of names and not as words that can be checked are counted as open too. The two digits are numerals and not counted. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 32 open letters at most, letters 52, 71, 79, 87, 95 to 119, 127, 135 and 143, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.

to Feigenhaus six hundred and eighteen Herkules reports on the twelfth, garbled of November that [at Nikolajew?] several steamers named in a list that is uncertain, the last one Operette of about [a number of?] tons have arrived

A short telegraph style report that several steamers of a stated tonnage have arrived at a Black Sea port. The names of the steamers and one letter of the date are uncertain, and one digit of the tonnage is open. The text is a best guess.
