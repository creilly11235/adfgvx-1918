# p059B

Childs page 56, 19 September 1918. Key sep19. Status Solved, 3 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading from two blind readings of the page, confidence reading with 3 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the page reading, 168 symbols where the log transcription shows 169, with the cells the readings do not settle left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 3 open letters at most, letters 43, 73 and 81, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
