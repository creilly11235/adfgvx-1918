# p177C

Childs page 174, 12 November 1918. Key nov10. Period key, as transcribed. Confidence high. Read with Lasry's published keys (Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker 2017).

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 174, which is page 177 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 177. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `C) CM v CZ (08.11 p.m ,Noveuber 12th)`. The heading counts no count symbols and the log shows 48.

## 2 Transcribed as printed

A dash is a symbol missing in the print.


## 3 Repaired or edited transcription

No edit. The transcription is used as it stands.

## 4 Decrypted as it stands

Key nov10, 16 columns. The printed transcription is decrypted with no repair. A letter that lost a symbol is `_`.

```
WANNRUECKKUNFTSEIFERTXRE
```

## 5 Decrypted after repair

Uppercase letters rest on symbols that are in the print. Lowercase letters rest on the repair. `_` is a letter not determined and `#` a digit not determined. X stands for a space or a stop.

```
WANNRUECKKUNFTSEIFERTXRE
```
