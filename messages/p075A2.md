# p075A2

Childs page 72, 6 October 1918. Key oct04. Status Partially solved. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading of 90 positions with 8 open positions, and 23 positions in 2 stretches that do not read. The ciphertext shown is the page reading with one copy of a doubled block of 25 cells removed. The page holds 251 cells against the stated count of 226, and the 25 extra cells repeat the 25 before them in every cell read in both, so the cipher clerk wrote that block twice. The second copy was removed, a choice made from the page and not by score. One cell of the removed copy is unread on the page, and letter 65 takes the read cell of the first copy in its place. Symbol 193 is read in the transcription but unread on both readings of the page image, so it is left unread. The text reads as German except at positions 36 to 40 and 76 to 93, 23 positions in all, which do not read under any of the repairs tried and are shown as undetermined. Inside the part that reads there are 8 open letters, letters 13, 18, 24, 35, 51, 53, 101 and 109, four on unread cells, three where another repair of the same block gives a different letter and one that does not fit its word. The round trip over every symbol of the text shown passes under the published key. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
