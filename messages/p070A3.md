# p070A3

Childs page 67, 5 October 1918. Key oct04. Status Solved, 27 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 27 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the collation of the two page witnesses, 220 symbols where the log transcription shows 223, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 27 open letters at most. 21 of them are located, letters 44 to 63 and 75, which the key determines but their context does not settle, and 6 more were counted by hand without a position, so the count is an upper bound and can fall. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
