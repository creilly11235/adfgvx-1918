# p107C2

Childs page 104, 2 November 1918. Key nov01. Period key, as transcribed. Confidence high. Read with Lasry's published keys (Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker 2017).

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 104, which is page 107 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 107. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `C) Pot v NKJ (9.10 a.m., November 4th)`, part 2. The heading counts 140 symbols and the log shows 140.

## 4 Decrypted as it stands

Key nov01, 19 columns. The printed transcription is decrypted with no repair. A letter that lost a symbol is `_`.

```

TERLXLTNXX
```

## 5 Decrypted after repair

Uppercase letters rest on symbols that are in the print. Lowercase letters rest on the repair. `_` is a letter not determined and `#` a digit not determined. X stands for a space or a stop.

```
DERGESUNDHEITZUMERSATZTRUPPJNTEILINMARSHGESETZTXXRETTERLXKET
TERLXLTNXX
```
