# p135B

Childs page 132, 5 November 1918. Key nov04. Status Solved, 23 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by Thomas and Norbert, 2017, in part.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 23 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the page reading, 155 cells, with one cell set aside in the round trip, so 154 symbols are shown. The printed count of 155 is odd, which a cipher of coordinate pairs cannot be, so one spare coordinate was removed as a first stage error, at a place that rests on the 2017 comments and a retest and not on the page. Two cells are unread on the page, and one more, which the page overstrikes, is left open. The first 14 letters do not resolve into words and are counted as open, together with the letters where the other close repairs decode differently. The repairs that add a coordinate instead read the same message shifted by one place and are not counted. The digit is read as a numeral and not counted. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 23 open letters at most, letters 1 to 14, 25 to 27, 29 to 33 and 52, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
