# p116D1

Childs page 113, 3 November 1918. Key nov01. Status Solved, 5 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 5 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the log transcription with the registered edits applied. The log transcription holds 362 cells against the printed count of 353, so a block of ten cells was left out, at a place chosen by score where several nearby places give almost the same text. That leaves 352 cells, so one cell of the printed 353 is still missing and the readings do not place it. Without the edit the page cells do not read, and most letters of the text depend on the block the score chose. No page reading has placed any of these edits. Letters where the other close repairs decode differently are counted as open. The digits are read as numerals and not counted. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 5 open letters at most, letters 9, 83, 150, 159 and 169, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
