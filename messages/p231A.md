# p231A

Childs page 228, 30 November 1918. Key nov28. Status Solved, 52 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 52 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the page reading, 374 symbols where the log transcription shows 386. A block of ten cells near the start was left out as an editorial repair, a statistical guess and not a page reading, so the opening counts as open. The later letters were also read on the stream with that guessed block removed. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 52 open letters at most, letters 1 to 45, 98, 106 to 107, 110, 128, 155 and 187, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
