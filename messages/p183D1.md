# p183D1

Childs page 180, ? November 1918. Key nov10. Status Solved, 20 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading from two blind readings of the page, confidence reading with 20 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the page reading, 196 symbols where the page shows one more, which is left out, with the cells the readings do not settle left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 20 open letters at most, letters 8, 10, 14, 18, 22, 30, 38, 40 to 41, 43, 49, 66, 73, 75, 81 to 82, 89 to 90, 94 and 97, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle, and two of the open cells may hide another wrong neighbouring cell, so the count could be higher. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
