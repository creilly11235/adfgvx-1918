# p127B2

Childs page 124, 4 November 1918. Key nov04. Period key, as transcribed. Confidence high. Read with Lasry's published keys (Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker 2017).

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 124, which is page 127 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 127. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `B) NKJ v LP (04.50 psm., November 4th)`, part 2. The heading counts 266 symbols and the log shows 266.

## 6 English translation

German in phrases.

```
A X O X K X 11 NSTBEAUFTRAGT X ZWEI GENERLSTABSOFFIZIERE ZU
DEN VERHANDLUNGEN ZU ENTSENDEN X WEISUNGEN AN A X J X K X 11
UND HIER 3 ER FUER DIE VERHANDLUNGEN ERBETEN X
```
