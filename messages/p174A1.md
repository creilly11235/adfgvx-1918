# p174A1

Childs page 171, 9 November 1918. Key nov07. Status Partially solved. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by Norbert, 2017, claimed.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading of the first 120 positions with 1 open position, then 37 positions that do not read. The ciphertext shown is the log transcription with the registered edits applied. The log transcription holds 315 cells against the printed count of 314 because one group has six symbols, and one symbol of that group was dropped. A block of five cells was then left out near the middle and five unknown cells were added near the end, a choice made by score where no page reading places either edit. Without these edits the page cells do not read, and most letters of the part that reads depend on the block that was left out. The first 120 positions read as German with 1 open letter, letter 114, and the round trip over every symbol of the text shown passes under the published key. The 37 positions after them do not read and are shown as undetermined. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
