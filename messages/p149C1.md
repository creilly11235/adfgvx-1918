# p149C1

Childs page 146, 6 November 1918. Key nov04. Status Solved, 10 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading from two blind readings of the page, confidence reading with 10 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the page reading, 176 symbols where the log transcription shows 367, with the cells the readings do not settle left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 10 open letters at most, letters 35, 43, 52, 60, 69, 76 and 85 to 88, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
