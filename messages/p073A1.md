# p073A1

Childs page 70, 6 October 1918. Key oct04. Status Solved, 20 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading from two blind readings of the page, confidence reading with 20 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the log transcription of 332 symbols with two repeated groups of five symbols removed, which brings it to the printed count of 322. Which two of the three repeated groups are removed was chosen by score, so the choice is not settled by the page, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 20 open letters at most, all located, letters 9, 20, 24, 31, 42, 53, 62, 64, 75, 86, 90, 97, 108, 116, 119, 127, 130, 141, 149 and 160, which the key determines but their context does not settle, and the digits are read as numerals. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
