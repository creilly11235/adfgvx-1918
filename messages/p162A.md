# p162A

Childs page 159, 8 November 1918. Key nov07. Status Solved, 19 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 19 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the page cells carried into the geometry of the twin, 259 of the 272 cells of this page, and the 13 cells of the page that fall outside the alignment are not used. This message was sent twice and its twin p171E is read after a length repair, so the page cells of this printing were carried into the geometry of the twin by an aligned transfer, and the 13 twin cells with no page counterpart are shown as unread. Of the 120 letters compared with the twin, 114 agree and 6 differ at cells where both printings give a determined symbol, and the five of them that are letters are counted as open. Twelve of the open cells take a fill that fits from the twin, and the digit that differs is read as it stands on this page. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over the 259 page cells shown passes under the published key. 19 open letters at most, letters 4, 11, 28, 37 to 38, 47 to 48, 57 to 60, 67 to 68, 77, 87, 97, 114, 132 and 134, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
