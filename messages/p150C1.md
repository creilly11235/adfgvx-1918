# p150C1

Childs page 147, 6 November 1918. Key nov04. Status Solved. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017.

Detail. Period key, as transcribed, confidence high.

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 147, which is page 150 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 150. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `C) YZ v LP (08.19 a.m., November 7th)`, part 1. The heading counts 96 symbols and the log shows 96.

## 3 Repaired or edited transcription

No edit. The transcription is used as it stands.

## 4 Decrypted as it stands

Key nov04, 17 columns. The printed transcription is decrypted with no repair. A letter that lost a symbol is `_`.

```
ANXDEUTSCHEBOTSCHAFTXPERAXXUNTERSCHRIFTXXSTUMMXX
```

## 5 Decrypted after repair

Uppercase letters rest on symbols that are in the print. Lowercase letters rest on the repair. `_` is a letter not determined and `#` a digit not determined. X stands for a space or a stop.

```
ANXDEUTSCHEBOTSCHAFTXPERAXXUNTERSCHRIFTXXSTUMMXX
```
