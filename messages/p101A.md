# p101A

Childs page 98, 1 November 1918. Key nov01. Status Solved. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017.

Detail. Period key, as transcribed, confidence high.

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 98, which is page 101 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 101. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `A) OSM v LP (-------, November lst)`. The heading counts no count symbols and the log shows 252.
