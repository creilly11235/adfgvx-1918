# p07943

Childs page 76, 28 October 1918. Key oct28. Status Solved, 3 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 3 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the log transcription with the registered edits applied. The log transcription holds 259 cells against the printed count of 238, so a block of twenty cells and the first cell were left out, at places chosen by score where nearby places give the same text. Without the edits the page cells do not read, and most letters of the text depend on the block the score chose. No page reading has placed any of these edits. Letters where the other close repairs decode differently are counted as open. The digits are read as numerals and not counted. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 3 open letters at most, letters 84, 105 and 117, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
