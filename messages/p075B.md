# p075B

Childs page 72, 6 October 1918. Key oct04. Status Solved, 1 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 1 open position. The ciphertext shown is the page reading, 274 symbols where the log transcription shows 276. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 1 open letter at most, letter 2, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
