# p116B9

Childs page 113, 3 November 1918. Key nov01. Period key, as transcribed. Confidence high. Read with Lasry's published keys (Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker 2017).

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 113, which is page 116 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 116. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `B) LP v NKJ (9.38 p.m., November 5th)`, part 9. The heading counts 216 symbols and the log shows 216.
