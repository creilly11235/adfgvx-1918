# p075A1

Childs page 72, 6 October 1918. Key oct04. Status Read, 1 open. Read with the published keys.

Detail. Known key reading from two blind readings of the page, confidence reading with 1 open position. Read with Lasry's published keys. The page symbols were read twice blind and agree on every letter, so the ciphertext shown is the agreed page reading. Open positions are marked in the text. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 72, which is page 75 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 75. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `A) COS v OSM (-------, October 9th)`, part 1. The heading counts 178 symbols and the log shows 204.
