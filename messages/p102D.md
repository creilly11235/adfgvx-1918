# p102D

Childs page 99, 1 November 1918. Key nov01. Period key, as transcribed. Confidence high. Read with Lasry's published keys (Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker 2017).

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 99, which is page 102 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 102. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `D) UKS v ZON (------ Novemver lst)`. The heading counts 100 symbols and the log shows 100.

## 3 Repaired or edited transcription

No edit. The transcription is used as it stands.

## 4 Decrypted as it stands

Key nov01, 19 columns. The printed transcription is decrypted with no repair. A letter that lost a symbol is `_`.

```
ANXOXHXLXXEINSXAXNRX11127XGAHXOPXERHALTENXEXOXKX11
```

## 5 Decrypted after repair

Uppercase letters rest on symbols that are in the print. Lowercase letters rest on the repair. `_` is a letter not determined and `#` a digit not determined. X stands for a space or a stop.

```
ANXOXHXLXXEINSXAXNRX11127XGAHXOPXERHALTENXEXOXKX11
```
