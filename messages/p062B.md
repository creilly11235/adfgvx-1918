# p062B

Childs page 59, 21 September 1918. Key sep19. Status Solved. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017.

Detail. Period key, length repaired, confidence high after repair.

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 59, which is page 62 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 62. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `B) OSM v LF (01.57 p.m. September 21st)`. The heading counts 172 symbols and the log shows 171.
