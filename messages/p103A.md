# p103A

Childs page 100, 1 November 1918. Key nov01. Status Solved, 7 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by Armin, 2017, claimed.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 7 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the page reading with the registered edits applied and the added cell marked as unknown. The page holds 122 cells, one of them unread, against the printed count of 123. One cell was dropped at cell 48 and one unknown cell added before cell 109, a pair chosen by score and not by the page, so the letters between them depend on that choice and no page reading has placed either edit. Without these two edits the page cells do not read. The three next best repairs, which add two cells instead, were decoded too and read the same message, differing at five letters besides the open cells, and those five are counted as open. The digit is read as a numeral and not counted. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 7 open letters at most, letters 11, 21, 24, 34, 43 and 52 to 53, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key.
