# p065A

Childs page 62, 4 October 1918. Key oct04. Period key, as transcribed. Confidence high. Read with Lasry's published keys (Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker 2017).

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 62, which is page 65 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 65. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `A) ASO v LP (04,50 pam., October 4th)`. The heading counts 124 symbols and the log shows 124.

Uppercase letters rest on symbols that are in the print. Lowercase letters rest on the repair. `_` is a letter not determined and `#` a digit not determined. X stands for a space or a stop.
