# p108BB

Childs page 105, 2 November 1918. Key nov01. Status Solved, 5 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by Norbert and Max Baertl, 2017.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 5 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the page reading with the registered edits applied and the added cell marked as unknown. One cell was dropped at cell 101 and one unknown cell added before cell 225, a pair chosen by score and not by the page, so the letters between them depend on that choice and no page reading has placed either edit. The other two registered places for the added cell, before cells 227 and 229, change two letters, which are counted as open together with the unknown cells they leave. The digits are read as numerals and not counted. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 5 open letters at most, letters 107, 116, 126, 135 and 145, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key.
