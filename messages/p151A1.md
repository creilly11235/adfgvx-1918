# p151A1

Childs page 148, 6 November 1918. Key nov04. Status Solved, 2 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 2 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the page reading, 130 symbols where the log transcription shows 125. Two readings of the page agree on all 130 cells, which equals the printed count, and the log transcription lacks five of them, so no edit was made. The seven digits are read as numerals and not counted. The two open letters are places where the word the text needs is not the letter the key gives. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 2 open letters at most, letters 10 and 32, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
