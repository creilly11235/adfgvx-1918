# p125A2

Childs page 122, 4 November 1918. Key nov04. Period key, as transcribed. Confidence high. Read with Lasry's published keys (Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker 2017).

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 122, which is page 125 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 125. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `A) UKS v LP (05.25 pim., November 4th)`, part 2. The heading counts 182 symbols and the log shows 182.

## 5 Decrypted after repair

Uppercase letters rest on symbols that are in the print. Lowercase letters rest on the repair. `_` is a letter not determined and `#` a digit not determined. X stands for a space or a stop.
