# p186A

Childs page 183, 12 November 1918. Key nov10. Status Solved, 2 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 2 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the collation of the two page witnesses, 64 symbols where the log transcription shows 66, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 2 open letters at most. One of them is located, letter 32, which the key determines but their context does not settle, and one more was counted by hand without a position, so the count is an upper bound and can fall. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
