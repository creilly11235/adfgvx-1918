# p227C2

Childs page 224, 26 November 1918. Key nov25. Status Solved, 12 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading from two blind readings of the page, confidence reading with 12 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the collation of the two page witnesses, 298 symbols where the log transcription shows 302, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 12 open letters at most. 4 of them are located, letters 101 to 104, which the key determines but their context does not settle, and 8 more were counted by hand without a position, so the count is an upper bound and can fall. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
