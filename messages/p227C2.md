# p227C2

Childs page 224, 26 November 1918. Key nov25. Known key fill on a page image reread. Confidence strong lead (margin 132.3). Read with Lasry's published keys.

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 224, which is page 227 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 227. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `C) UKS v BSL (00.13 a.m., Novenber 28th)`, part 2. The heading counts 298 symbols and the log shows 302.
