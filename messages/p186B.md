# p186B

Childs page 183, 12 November 1918. Key nov10. Status Read, 7 open. Read with the published keys.

Detail. Known key reading from two blind readings of the page, confidence reading with 7 open positions. Read with Lasry's published keys. The page symbols were read twice blind and agree on every letter, so the ciphertext shown is the agreed page reading. Open positions are marked in the text. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
