# p170A

Childs page 167, 9 November 1918. Key nov07. Status Solved, 9 open. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017. Solution published by this project, 2026.

Detail. Known key reading by a model read of the decode, confidence reading with 9 open positions. The ciphertext shown is the page reading, 204 symbols where the log transcription shows 200. This is the one ciphertext printed on both pages 169 and 170, read as a joint record from the readings of both printings, and the page 169 printing has 20 cells fewer at the start. The two registered values at cells 141 to 143 are both supported and neither is chosen, so the three letters where they differ are counted as open. Cells the readings do not settle are left open, and the round trip over every symbol of it passes under the published key. 9 open letters at most, letters 2, 14, 28, 30, 40, 50, 60, 85 and 95, which the key determines but the page or their context does not settle. Read with a published key, and whether anyone read it earlier is not confirmed.
