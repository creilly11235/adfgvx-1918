# p169C3

Childs page 166, 9 November 1918. Key nov07. Status Solved. Key found by Lasry, Niebel, Kopal and Wacker, 2017.

Detail. Period key, as transcribed, confidence high.

## 1 Printed

J. Rives Childs, *General Solution of the ADFGVX Cipher System*, Aegean Park Press, Cryptographic Series 88 (ISBN 0894122843), Appendix B, the intercept logs of 1918. The scanned book is the free full text PDF of 248 pages offered by the Aegean Park Press index kept by Richard Bean. The message is on printed page 166, which is page 169 of the PDF. Open the [PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1_JiNMck7eUvdrOTrH7fsj1Yh44t-VSC6/view?usp=sharing) and go to PDF page 169. The index is [here](https://richardbean.id.au/misc/kryptos/aegean-park-press.html). Page images are not republished here.

Heading in the log, `C) UKS v NKJ (9.47 a.m., November 9th)`, part 3. The heading counts 100 symbols and the log shows 100.

## 4 Decrypted as it stands

Key nov07, 20 columns. The printed transcription is decrypted with no repair. A letter that lost a symbol is `_`.

```

```

## 5 Decrypted after repair

Uppercase letters rest on symbols that are in the print. Lowercase letters rest on the repair. `_` is a letter not determined and `#` a digit not determined. X stands for a space or a stop.

```

```
